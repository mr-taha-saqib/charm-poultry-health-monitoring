About Us

Welcome to the About Us page! At CHARM (Chicken Health Assessment and Remote Monitoring), we use cutting-edge AI to revolutionize poultry disease diagnosis and management. Our focus is on combating major diseases like infectious coryza, fowl cholera, and fowl pox, as well as addressing general health concerns in chickens. By providing fast, easy-to-use diagnostic tools, we empower farmers to protect their flocks, reduce losses, and ensure a sustainable food supply. Together, we’re transforming poultry health care for a healthier, more efficient industry.
